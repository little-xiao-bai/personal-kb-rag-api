Lewis lamp

The lamp used a similar design to an Argand lamp, adding a parabolic reflector behind the lamp and a magnifying lens made from 4 in green bottle glass in front of the lamp.

A similar variant using a parabolic reflector was created by the inventor of the Argand lamp, Aimé Argand.

While the Argand variant became widely used by European lighthouses, the Lewis lamp design was selected by the United States for use in American lighthouses.
