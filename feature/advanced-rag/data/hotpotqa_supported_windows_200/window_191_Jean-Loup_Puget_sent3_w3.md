Jean-Loup Puget

Jean-Loup Puget and his collaborators reported the first identification of the Cosmic infrared background using COBE data.

He is also, along with Alain Léger, credited with the origin of the hypothesis that the series of infrared lines observed in numerous astrophysical objects are caused by emission from polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons.

He is currently principal investigator of the HFI module of the Planck space mission.
