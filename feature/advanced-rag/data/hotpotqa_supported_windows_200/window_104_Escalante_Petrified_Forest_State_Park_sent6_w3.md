Escalante Petrified Forest State Park

The wood is multicolored (mostly red, yellow, white, and black) and was prized by hobbyists before the Park was established.

The logs are believed to be of conifers that were transported by a river before being buried and later fossilized as agate.

A 50 foot (15m) log is displayed near the trail head and is one of the most complete fossil logs known from the Morrison Formation.
