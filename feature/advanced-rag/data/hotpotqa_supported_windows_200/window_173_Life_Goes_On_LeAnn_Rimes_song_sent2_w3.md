Life Goes On (LeAnn Rimes song)

The song did not make it into the Hot 100 in the US, but it did make number 9 on the "Billboard" Hot Adult Contemporary Tracks chart, and was a bigger hit outside America.

It reached the top 10 in Australia, the Netherlands and Sweden, and broke the top 5 in New Zealand, peaking at number 4.

It peaked within the top 20 in the United Kingdom and Norway.
