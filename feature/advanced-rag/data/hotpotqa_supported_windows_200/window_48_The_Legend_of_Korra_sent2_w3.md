The Legend of Korra

It was created by Bryan Konietzko and Michael Dante DiMartino as a sequel to "", which aired from 2005 to 2008.

Animated in a style strongly influenced by anime, the series is set in a fictional universe in which some people can manipulate, or "bend", the elements of water, earth, fire, or air.

Only one person, the "Avatar", can bend all four elements, and is responsible for maintaining balance in the world.
