God Bless America (LeAnn Rimes album)

"Put a Little Holiday in Your Heart" was originally released as a bonus single, with the song "Unchained Melody", alongside "Blue" at Target during the 1996 Christmas season.

The only two new songs that were released on the album were "The Lord's Prayer" and "The Sands of Time".

The album was released as a patriotic tribute to the events of September 11, 2001, with the liner stating "These classic recordings were made while America was first discovering LeAnn Rimes."
