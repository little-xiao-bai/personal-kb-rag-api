Bachelor of Pharmacy

In some countries, it has been superseded by the Doctor of Pharmacy (PharmD) and Master of Pharmacy (MPharm) degrees.

In the United States, this degree was granted as the baccalaureate pharmacy degree only at Washington State University, where it has now been superseded by the PharmD degree.

The degree previously offered within the US—and still the required degree in Canada—is the Bachelor of Science in Pharmacy.
