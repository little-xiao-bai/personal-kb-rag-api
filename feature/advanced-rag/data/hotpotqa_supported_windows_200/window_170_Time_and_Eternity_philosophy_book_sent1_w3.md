Time and Eternity (philosophy book)

Time and Eternity - An Essay on the Philosophy of Religion (1st imp.

Princeton New Jersey 1952, Princeton University Press, 169 pp) is a philosophy book written by Walter Terence Stace.

At the time of writing, Stace was a professor of philosophy at Princeton University, where he had worked since 1932 after a 22-year career in the Ceylon Civil Service.
