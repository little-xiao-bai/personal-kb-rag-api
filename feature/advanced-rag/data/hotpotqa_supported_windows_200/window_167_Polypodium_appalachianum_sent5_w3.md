Polypodium appalachianum

In 1991, it was resolved that the type of "P. virginianum" was the tetraploid series, and that it is an allotetraploid species of hybrid origin, with the diploid species as one parent.

The diploid species was then named "P. appalachianum".

The other parent of "P. virginianum" was found to be "Polypodium sibiricum".
