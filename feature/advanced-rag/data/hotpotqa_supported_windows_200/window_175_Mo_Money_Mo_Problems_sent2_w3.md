Mo Money Mo Problems

Released posthumously, the single topped the "Billboard" Hot 100 for two weeks in 1997, replacing "I'll Be Missing You" from the chart, Puff Daddy's tribute to the rapper's death himself.

The song is Notorious B.I.G.'s second posthumous number one single, following "Hypnotize", making him the only artist in Hot 100 history to have two #1 singles posthumously.

It was the sixth song to hit #1 posthumously for a credited artist.
