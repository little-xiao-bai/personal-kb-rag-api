Fortaleza Metro

The Fortaleza Metro (Portuguese: "Metrô de Fortaleza" , commonly called "Metrofor") is the meter gauge 43 km metropolitan rail system that operates in the city of Fortaleza in Ceará, Brazil.

It is operated by Companhia Cearense Metropolitan Transport, which was founded in May 2, 1997, and is responsible for the management, construction and subway system planning in the city of Fortaleza and its metropolitan area.
