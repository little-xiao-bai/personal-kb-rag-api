Yasuda Dai Circus

The three are much less about traditional skit or story based stand-up humor (which is common in Japanese comedy), choosing instead to focus on physical humor and a loud, boisterous style that resonates with most manzai audiences.

They formed in 2001, and received their name from owarai "kombi" Masuda Okada's Kisuke Masuda in parody of the famous "Kinoshita Dai Circus".

The group's name simply means "Great Yasuda Circus".
