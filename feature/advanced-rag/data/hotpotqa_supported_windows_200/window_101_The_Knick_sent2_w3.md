The Knick

The series follows Dr. John W. Thackery (Clive Owen) and the staff at a fictionalized version of the Knickerbocker Hospital (the "Knick") in New York during the early twentieth century.

Amiel and Begler write the majority of the episodes and are executive producers.

Owen, Soderbergh, Gregory Jacobs, and Michael Sugar (Anonymous Content) are also executive producers.
