Booty Luv

Booty Luv (originally called Booty Luv and Cherise & Nadia), were a British dance group formed in June 2006 via the Hed Kandi label.

The group comprises two R&B vocalists, Cherise Roberts and Nadia Shepherd, both of whom were in the original line-up of the hip hop and R&B group Big Brovaz.
