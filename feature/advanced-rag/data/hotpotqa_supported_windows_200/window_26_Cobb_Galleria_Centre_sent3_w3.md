Cobb Galleria Centre

It has hosted over 15,000 events and millions of guests.

The venue operates under the direction of the Cobb-Marietta Coliseum and Exhibit Hall Authority and is located at the intersection of three major highways: Interstate 75, Interstate 285, and Cobb Parkway (U.S. 41) just northwest of the city.

The Galleria Specialty Mall, which pre-dates the convention center, is located downstairs, with meeting halls upstairs.
