Ron and Tammys

In the episode, Ron Swanson (Nick Offerman) is confronted by his first ex-wife, Tammy 1 (Patricia Clarkson) who has a malevolent influence on him, and Leslie Knope (Amy Poehler) becomes involved in Ron's personal life.

This episode marks the first and only appearance of Ron's mother, Tamara (Paula Pell).

It garnered 4.33 million viewers, an increase in viewers from the previous episode's 4.11 million.
