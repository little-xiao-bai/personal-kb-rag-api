José Graziano da Silva

José Graziano da Silva (born November 17, 1949) is a Brazilian American agronomist and writer.

As a scholar, he has authored several books about the problems of agriculture in Brazil.

Between 2003 and 2004, Graziano served in the Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva cabinet as Extraordinary Minister for Food Security, being responsible for implementing the Fome Zero (Zero Hunger) program, which took 28 million people out of the national poverty line during the 8 years of the Lula administration .
