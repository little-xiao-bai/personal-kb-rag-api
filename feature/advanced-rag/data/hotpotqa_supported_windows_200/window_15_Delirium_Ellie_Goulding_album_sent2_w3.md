Delirium (Ellie Goulding album)

Music critics were generally impressed by the overall production of the record, although they were ambivalent in regards to its originality.

It debuted at number three on the UK Albums Chart and the US "Billboard" 200, earning Goulding her highest-charting record in the latter country and her highest first-week sales figures in both territories.

The album spawned three singles: "On My Mind", "Army" and "Something in the Way You Move".
