List of Romeo × Juliet episodes

"Romeo × Juliet" (ロミオ×ジュリエット , Romio to Jurietto ) is a 24-episode anime television adaptation of "Romeo and Juliet", a 16th-century tragedy originally written by William Shakespeare.

The English playwright himself makes a cameo appearance in the series as a minor character.

The series was produced jointly by Gonzo and SKY Perfect Well Think, with Fumitoshi Oizaki as the main director.
