Monster Force

The story is set in approx. 2020 and centers on a group of teenagers who, with help of high tech weaponry, fight off against classic Universal Monsters and spiritual beings threatening humanity.

Some of the crew have personal vendettas (e.g., one has the ""curse of the Wolfman"" that has been handed down through generations and another had a family member taken away from her by Dracula), while others fight for Mankind out of a sense of altruism.

The series aired in syndication alongside another Universal animated series, "Exosquad".
