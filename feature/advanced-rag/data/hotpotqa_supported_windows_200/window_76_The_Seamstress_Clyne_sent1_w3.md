The Seamstress (Clyne)

The Seamstress is a concerto for solo violin and orchestra by the British-born composer Anna Clyne.

The work was commissioned by the Chicago Symphony Orchestra, for which Clyne was then composer-in-residence.

It was first performed May 28, 2015 at Symphony Center, Chicago by the violinist Jennifer Koh and the Chicago Symphony Orchestra under conductor Ludovic Morlot.
