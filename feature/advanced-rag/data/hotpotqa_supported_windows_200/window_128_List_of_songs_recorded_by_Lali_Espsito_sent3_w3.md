List of songs recorded by Lali Espósito

From 2007 to 2012, the singer was part of the pop-group Teen Angels, derived from the television series "Casi Ángeles" in which she also starred from the same from 2007 to 2010.

In 2013 Espósito also began to work as an independent artist outside of Teen Angels.

Later that year, the singer released four songs for the Argentine telenovela Solamente Vos in which she starred.
