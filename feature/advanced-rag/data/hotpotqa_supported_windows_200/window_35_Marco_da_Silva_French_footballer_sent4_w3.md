Marco da Silva (French footballer)

He helped the team earn promotion to the Championnat de France amateur in the 2010–11 season and is the team's current captain.

Da Silva made his professional debut with Valenciennes on 31 August 2011 against Dijon in the Coupe de la Ligue.

He started the match and was substituted out after 73 minutes as Valenciennes were defeated 3–2.
