KTV Ltd.

Founded in 1980 by Sharon and Mario Zuvic Bulic, KTV Ltd. receives channels from the UK, US, Saint Helena Island, and Chile and rebroadcasts them to subscribers in the Falkland Islands.

KTV Ltd. cooperates with a number of other small radio broadcasting companies in other British overseas territories, notably Saint FM in St Helena, the Falkland Islands Radio Service (FIRS), and Myriam's Country.
