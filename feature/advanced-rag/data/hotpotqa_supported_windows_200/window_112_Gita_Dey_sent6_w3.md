Gita Dey

She acted in the movie "Teen Kanya" under the direction of Satyajit Roy and Rittik Ghatak's "Meghe Dhaka Tara, Subarnarekha, Komal Gandhar, Kato Ajanare".

She also acted in Hindi movies such as "Parineeta" (2005) with Vidya Balan and Sanjay Dutt and other movies.

She was associated with All India Radio for a long time doing Shruti Natok.
