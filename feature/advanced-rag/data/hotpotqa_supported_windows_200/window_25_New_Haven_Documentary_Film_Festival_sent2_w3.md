New Haven Documentary Film Festival

Screenings take place at Yale University’s Whitney Humanities Center Auditorium and the New Haven Free Public Library.

NHdocs is a regional festival that showcases documentaries by filmmakers from the greater New Haven area and beyond.

NHdocs was launched in 2014 when the film festival’s co-founders Charles Musser, Gorman Bechard, Jacob Bricca, and Lisa Molomot came together at the Big Sky Documentary Film Festival and decided to create a documentary film festival in New Haven that would “build a sense of community among documentary filmmakers from the greater New Haven area.”
