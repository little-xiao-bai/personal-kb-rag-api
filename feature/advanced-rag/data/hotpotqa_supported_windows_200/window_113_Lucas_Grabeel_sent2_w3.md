Lucas Grabeel

He is best known for roles such as Ryan Evans in the "High School Musical" film series, as Ezra Chase in The Adventures of Food Boy (2008) and as Ethan Dalloway in the third and fourth installments of the Halloweentown series: "Halloweentown High" (2004) and "Return to Halloweentown" (2006).

He also appeared as a young Lex Luthor and Conner Kent in the television series "Smallville".

He played Toby Kennish in the ABC Family/Freeform drama "Switched at Birth".
