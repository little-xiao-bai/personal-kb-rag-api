European route E47

European route E 47 is a highway going from Lübeck in Germany via Copenhagen, Denmark to Helsingborg, Sweden, which is also known under the name Vogelfluglinie and Sydmotorvejen.

The road has motorway standard all the way except for 28 km in Germany, the part inside Helsingør (6 km/4 miles city street), and there are also two ferry connections.

The route has a ferry connection between Germany and Denmark.
