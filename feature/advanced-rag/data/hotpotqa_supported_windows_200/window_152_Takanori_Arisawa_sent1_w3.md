Takanori Arisawa

Takanori Arisawa (有澤 孝紀 , Arisawa Takanori , April 2, 1951 – November 26, 2005) was a Japanese composer and arranger best known for composing the "Sailor Moon" anime series and "Digimon" series (Seasons 1-4).

He wrote music for the series, including its video games.

Born in Tokyo, Arisawa began to learn piano at the age of 20.
