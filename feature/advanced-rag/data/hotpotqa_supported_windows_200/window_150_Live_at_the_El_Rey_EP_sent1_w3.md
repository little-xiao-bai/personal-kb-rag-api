Live at the El Rey (EP)

Live at the El Rey was a limited edition live album recorded by New Orleans electro-rock band Mutemath.

The album was recorded live at the El Rey Theatre in Los Angeles on the Album Release Tour in January 2006 and features a selection of six songs from the actual set performed on the Album Release Tour.

Video of the performances was also recorded and used for promotional materials on various online media outlets including AOL Music and was included in the UK physical release of the single "Typical".
