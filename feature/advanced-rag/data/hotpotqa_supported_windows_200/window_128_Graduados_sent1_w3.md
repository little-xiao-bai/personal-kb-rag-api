Graduados

Graduados (English: The Graduates ) is a 2012 Argentine telenovela which was broadcast by Telefe from March 12 to December 19.

The plot concerns a group of people who graduated from high school in 1989 and reunite twenty years later.

The main character, Andrés Goddzer (Daniel Hendler), discovers that María Laura Falsini (Nancy Dupláa) was pregnant in 1989 and married Pablo Catáneo (Luciano Cáceres), who thought that he was the child's father.
