List of Avatar: The Last Airbender episodes

"" is a 61-episode American animated television series created by Michael Dante DiMartino and Bryan Konietzko.

It first aired on February 21, 2005, on Nickelodeon with a one-hour series premiere and concluded its run with a two-hour TV movie on July 19, 2008.

The "Avatar: The Last Airbender" franchise refers to each season as a "Book", in which each episode is referred to as a "chapter".
