Live at the El Rey (EP)

Video of the performances was also recorded and used for promotional materials on various online media outlets including AOL Music and was included in the UK physical release of the single "Typical".

Only 25,000 copies of the EP were printed and sold as part of an exclusive limited edition version of the group's self-titled debut album "Mutemath" when it was re-released in the US on Warner Bros.

Records on September 26, 2006.
