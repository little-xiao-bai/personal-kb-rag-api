List of songs recorded by Ellie Goulding

The first single released from the album was "Under the Sheets", which Goulding wrote in collaboration with Starsmith.

Starsmith co-wrote four other songs and served as the album's primary producer.

Goulding also collaborated with Jonny Lattimer on the singles "Starry Eyed" and "The Writer", and shared writing credits with Fraser T Smith on "Your Biggest Mistake".
