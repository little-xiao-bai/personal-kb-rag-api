List of accolades received by Carol (film)

Sarah Paulson, Kyle Chandler, and Jake Lacy feature in supporting roles.

"Carol" premiered in May at the 2015 Cannes Film Festival, where it won the Queer Palm and Mara tied for the Best Actress award.

It received a platform release in the United States, opening in four theaters on November 20, 2015 and going into wide release on January 15, 2016.
