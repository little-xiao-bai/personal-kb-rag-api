List of Avatar: The Last Airbender episodes

The "Avatar: The Last Airbender" franchise refers to each season as a "Book", in which each episode is referred to as a "chapter".

Each "Book" takes its name from one of the elements that Aang, the protagonist, must master: Water, Earth, and Fire.

The show's first two seasons each consisted of 20 episodes, while the third season had 21.
