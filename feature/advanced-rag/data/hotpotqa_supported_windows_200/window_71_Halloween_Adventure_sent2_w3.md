Halloween Adventure

The company was founded in 1981 by brothers Bruce and Darron Goldman in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania and has locations in Pennsylvania, New York, New Jersey, Delaware, Maryland, Florida, and California.

In 2006 there were over 120 "seasonal stores", using temporary locations in shopping malls that were vacated for seasonal short-term rentals.

Halloween Adventure sells a variety of costumes, masks, makeup, props, hats and other accessories for adults and children.
