Emirate of Afghanistan

The Emirate of Afghanistan (Pashto: د افغانستان امارت‎ "Da Afghānistān Amārat ") was an emirate between Central Asia and South Asia, which is today's Islamic Republic of Afghanistan.

The emirate emerged from the Durrani Empire, when Dost Mohammed Khan, the founder of the Barakzai dynasty in Kabul, prevailed.
