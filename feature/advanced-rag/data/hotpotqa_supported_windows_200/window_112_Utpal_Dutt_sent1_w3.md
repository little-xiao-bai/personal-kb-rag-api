Utpal Dutt

Utpal Dutta (Bengali: "উৎপল দত্ত" "Utpôl Dôtto",    ) (29 March 1929 – 19 August 1993) was an Indian actor, director, and writer-playwright.

He was primarily an actor in Bengali theatre, where he became a pioneering figure in Modern Indian theatre, when he founded the "Little Theater Group" in 1949.

This group enacted many English, Shakespearean and Brecht plays, in a period now known as the "Epic theater" period, before it immersed itself completely in highly political and radical theatre.
