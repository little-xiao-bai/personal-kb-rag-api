Selver

Expansion outside of Tallinn began on 10 May 2002 with the opening of Mai Selver in Pärnu.

Since 18 December 2008 Selver also operated in Latvia, but in the end of 2009 due to the financial crisis Selver was forced to close all its 6 supermarkets in Latvia.

There are currently 44 stores in operation all over Estonia (as of December 2014).
