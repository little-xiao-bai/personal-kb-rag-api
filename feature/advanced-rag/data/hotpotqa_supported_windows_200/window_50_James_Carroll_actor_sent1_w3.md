James Carroll (actor)

James Carroll (December 20, 1955 – April 27, 2016) was an American-born Canadian actor and radio personality, best known for playing Max Sutton on "Wind at My Back", which aired on CBC Television from 1996 to 2001.

Most recently, Carroll found a second career as a community radio host and personality based in Huntsville, Ontario.

Carroll initially joined Hunters Bay Radio (CKAR-FM) in 2010 as the host of a local afternoon radio show.
