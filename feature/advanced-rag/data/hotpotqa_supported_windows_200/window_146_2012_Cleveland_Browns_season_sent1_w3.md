2012 Cleveland Browns season

The Cleveland Browns season was the team's 64th season as a professional sports franchise and its 60th season as a member of the National Football League (NFL).

Although the team improved on its record to 5–11 this 2012 season from its 4–12 finish in 2011, the team still placed fourth in the AFC North.

The team also failed to break its 9-year playoff drought, the longest in franchise history.
