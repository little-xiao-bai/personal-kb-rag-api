McLean, Virginia

McLean is home to many diplomats, businessmen, members of Congress, and high-ranking government officials partially due to its proximity to Washington, D.C. and the Central Intelligence Agency.

It is the location of Hickory Hill, the former home of Ethel Kennedy, the widow of Robert F. Kennedy.

It is also the location of Salona, the former home of Light-Horse Harry Lee, the Revolutionary War hero.
