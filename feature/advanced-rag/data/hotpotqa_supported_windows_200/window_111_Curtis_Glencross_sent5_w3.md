Curtis Glencross

After splitting the 2014–15 season between Calgary and Washington, Glencross was unable to reach a contract deal with any NHL team (despite pre-season attempts with the Toronto Maple Leafs and Colorado Avalanche and opted to retire as a player.

During his playing career, Glencross was a spokesman for the Special Olympics.

He has also participated with Rae Croteau Jr.'s chuckwagon racing team in the summer.
