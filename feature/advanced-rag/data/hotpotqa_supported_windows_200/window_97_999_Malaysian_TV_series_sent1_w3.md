999 (Malaysian TV series)

999 (read in Malay as "sembilan sembilan sembilan") is a Malaysian investigative reality television series show (similar to "COPS" in the United States); which focusing to crimes in the country.

The show has aired on TV3 since 2004 and has aired every Tuesday at 9:00 pm since January 2014.

Previously it aired every Thursday at 9:00 pm since it first aired.
