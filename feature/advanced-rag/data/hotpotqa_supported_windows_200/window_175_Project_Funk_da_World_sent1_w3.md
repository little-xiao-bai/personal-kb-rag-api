Project Funk da World

Project: Funk da World is the debut studio album by rapper Craig Mack, released September 20, 1994.

The album was the second release on Bad Boy Records, following The Notorious B.I.G.'s classic "Ready to Die" by one week.

Propelled by the success of the Platinum RIAA-selling smash hit single "Flava in Ya Ear", the album reached Gold-RIAA sales status on February 22, 1995.
