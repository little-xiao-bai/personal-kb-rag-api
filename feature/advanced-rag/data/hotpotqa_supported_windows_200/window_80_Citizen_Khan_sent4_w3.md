Citizen Khan

"Citizen Khan" follows the trials and tribulations of Mr Khan, a loud-mouthed, patriarchal, cricket-loving, self-appointed community leader, and his long suffering wife (played by Shobu Kapoor) and daughters Shazia (Maya Sondhi 2012–2014, Krupa Pattani 2015–) and Alia (Bhavna Limbachia).

In Series One, Kris Marshall starred as Dave, the manager of Mr Khan's local mosque.

The first name of Mrs Khan is Razia, however Mr Khan's first name is never revealed.
