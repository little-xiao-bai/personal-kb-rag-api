Essex dialect

The Essex dialect is a rapidly disappearing dialect similar to some forms of East Anglian English and is now mainly confined to the north and the east of Essex.

It shares vast similarities with both Suffolk and Norfolk dialects, with its own peculiarities.

With rapid urbanisation in the twentieth century as well as the impact of the London overspill, Estuary English, a milder form of the London accent predominant largely along the Thames Estuary and thus the name, has become common, mainly in the southern portion of the county.
