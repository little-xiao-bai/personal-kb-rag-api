Al Wilson (offensive lineman)

He is a member of the Canadian Football Hall of Fame, the B.C. Sports Hall of Fame, the B.C. Lions Wall of Fame, and has a street named in his honor, "Al Wilson Grove," in his hometown of Duncan.

Wilson's #52 jersey is one of eight numbers retired by the B.C. Lions.

In 2003, Wilson was voted a member of the B.C. Lions All-Time Dream Team as part of the club’s 50th anniversary celebration.
