Jens Ritter Instruments

Jens Ritter Instruments is a manufacturer of high-end electric stringed instruments.

It was founded by Jens Ritter in the mid-1990s and was known as Ritter Bass Guitars until 2010 when Jens expanded his line to include guitars.

Jens produces 50 to 60 handmade instruments each year in his shop located in the small wine town of Deidesheim, Germany.
