Summit Air Flight 409

On May 27, 2017, Summit Air flight 409, a Let L-410, crashed short of the runway threshold whilst attempting landing at Tenzing–Hillary Airport in Nepal.

It was on final approach to Lukla's runway 06 at about 1404 Local Time when the aircraft hit trees short of the runway and subsequently crashed on the ground 3 m below runway level.

The aircraft the slid down the slope before coming to a rest about 200 m below runway level.
