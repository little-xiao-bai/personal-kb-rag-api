9 to 5 (musical)

It received 15 Drama Desk Award nominations, the most received by a production in a single year, as well as four Tony Awards nominations.

The Broadway production however was short-lived, closing in September 2009.

A national tour of the US launched in 2010, followed by a UK premiere in 2012 and returns to the UK in 2017 in a new production at The Gatehouse in London with a West End cast, as part of a fringe festival.
