Extensibility

Extensions can be through the addition of new functionality or through modification of existing functionality.

The central theme is to provide for change – typically enhancements – while minimizing impact to existing system functions.

An extensible system is one whose internal structure and data flow are minimally or not affected by new or modified functionality, for example recompiling or changing the original source code might be unnecessary when changing a system’s behavior, either by the creator or other programmers.
