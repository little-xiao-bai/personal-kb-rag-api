Cosmos: A Spacetime Odyssey

The show is presented by astrophysicist Neil deGrasse Tyson, who, as a young high school student, was inspired by Sagan.

Among the executive producers are Seth MacFarlane, whose financial investment was instrumental in bringing the show to broadcast television, and Ann Druyan, a co-author and co-creator of the original television series and Sagan's wife.

The show is produced by Brannon Braga, and Alan Silvestri composed the backing score.
