Victory (Puff Daddy song)

It features The Notorious B.I.G., who raps two verses, and Busta Rhymes, who raps the song's chorus.

The song also heavily sampled the Bill Conti song "Going the Distance", which featured on the soundtrack to the movie "Rocky" making it a darker start to a rap album that featured many (at the time) club-standard singles.

The song was released as a single in 1998, peaking at number 19 on the "Billboard" Hot 100.
