Robin Goldstein

He is author of several books, including "The Wine Trials" (the world's bestselling guide to cheap wine), "The Beer Trials", and an upcoming book tentatively entitled "A Defense of Fast Food".

Goldstein was also one of the subjects of "Think Like a Freak", the 2014 book by "Freakonomics" authors Steven Levitt and Stephen Dubner.

He lives in Oakland, California.
