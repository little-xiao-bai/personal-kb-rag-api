North American T-6 Texan

The United States Army Air Corps (USAAC) and USAAF designated it as the AT-6, the United States Navy the SNJ, and British Commonwealth air forces the Harvard, the name by which it is best known outside of the US.

Starting in 1948, the new United States Air Force (USAF) designated it the T-6, with the USN following in 1962.

It remains a popular warbird aircraft used for airshow demonstrations and static displays.
