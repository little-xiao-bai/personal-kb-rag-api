Arabs in India

Many Sufi Saints such as Abdul-Qadir Gilani and Moinuddin Chishti and their descendants claim themselves as "Najeeb AlTarfayn" although many genealogists dispute this fact.

Sunni Sheikhs also claim Arab descent from Sufis or migrants.

They don't know their tribe but trace lineage from Umar - Farooqi, Abu Bakr- Siddiqui, Uthman - Usmani and Ali - Alawi or Mir, who established the Rashidun Caliphate.
