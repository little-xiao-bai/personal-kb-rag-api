United States v. Quiver

The district court had held that adultery committed by an Indian with another Indian on an Indian reservation was not punishable under the act of March 3, 1887, c. 397, 24 Stat.

635, now § 316 of the Penal Code.

This decision was made because the offense occurred on a Sioux Indian reservation which is not said to be under jurisdiction of the district court.
