Guelph Royals (baseball)

From 1861 to 1925, the team divided its home games between various ballparks in the city, before the construction of a stadium in Exhibition Park led them to permanently play there.

In 1986, they moved into a new stadium, David E. Hastings Stadium, also in Exhibition Park.

In spite of only winning one Intercounty championship between 1932 and 1993, the team has done well in recent years, winning four since.
