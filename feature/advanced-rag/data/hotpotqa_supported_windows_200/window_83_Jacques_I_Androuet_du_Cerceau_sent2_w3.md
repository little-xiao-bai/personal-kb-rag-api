Jacques I Androuet du Cerceau

He introduced Renaissance architecture to France with the assistance of Pierre Lescot, Philibert Delorme and Jean Bullant.

Though he was referred to by contemporaries as "architecte" and was even appointed "architecte du roi", he is remembered especially for his suites of engravings produced from 1549 (beginning with a suite of Triumphal arches) from his printshop in Orléans.
