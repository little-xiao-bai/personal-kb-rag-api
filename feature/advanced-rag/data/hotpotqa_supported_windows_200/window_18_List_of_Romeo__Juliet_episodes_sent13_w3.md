List of Romeo × Juliet episodes

"Inori: You Raise Me Up" by Lena Park was used as a special ending for episode 24.

The anime is licensed in North America by Funimation.

The complete series was released in two sections, with the first half of the series, the "Romeo Collection" being released June 23, 2009 and the second half of the series, the "Juliet Collection" being released August 11, 2009.
