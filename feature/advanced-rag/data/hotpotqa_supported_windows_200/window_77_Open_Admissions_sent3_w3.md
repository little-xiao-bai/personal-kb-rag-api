The Broadway production starred Calvin Levels as Calvin Jefferson, Marilyn Rockafellow as Ginny Carlsen, Nan-Lynn Nelson as Salina Jones, Pam Potillo as Georgia Jones, and Sloane Shelton as Professor Clare Block.

The play depicts the travails of a professor at a second-rate college, including an unsupportive husband.

Levels won a Theatre World Award and was nominated for both a Tony Award and Drama Desk Award for his performance.
