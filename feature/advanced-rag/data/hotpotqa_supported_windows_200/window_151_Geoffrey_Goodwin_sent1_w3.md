Geoffrey Goodwin

Geoffrey Goodwin is a television and digital executive who has been active for 20 years in both the UK industry and his native Canada.

Geoffrey was Head of Strategy for Drama, Entertainment, Comedy, Children’s and Film at the BBC from 2003 to 2007.

He also wrote the BBC’s under 18s strategy with Andy Parfitt that helped set out the case for the BBC’s license fee negotiations in 2007.
