No Promises (Icehouse song)

"No Promises" is the first single released by Australian band, Icehouse from the band's 1986 album, "Measure for Measure" and was released in November 1985 on Regular Records as 7" Vinyl Single and 12" Vinyl Single formats.

It was also released in the UK and Europe by Chrysalis Records again on 7" and 12" formats but with different track listings.
