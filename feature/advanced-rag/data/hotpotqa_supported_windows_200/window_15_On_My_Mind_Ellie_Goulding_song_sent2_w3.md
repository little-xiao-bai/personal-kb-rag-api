On My Mind (Ellie Goulding song)

It was released as the album's lead single on 17 September 2015.

It was written by Goulding, Max Martin, Savan Kotecha and Ilya Salmanzadeh.

"On My Mind" is an electropop and R&B song whose instrumentation consists of scratchy guitars, trap drums, slapped beats and sharp, syncopated electronica.
