Cedric the Entertainer

He also hosted BET's "ComicView" during the 1993–1994 season and "Def Comedy Jam" in 1995.

He is best known for co-starring with Steve Harvey on The WB sitcom "The Steve Harvey Show" and starring as Eddie Walker in "Barbershop".

He hosted the twelfth season of daytime version of "Who Wants to Be a Millionaire" in the 2013–2014 television season.
