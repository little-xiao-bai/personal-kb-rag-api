Viaport Rotterdam

The mall has an area of 900000 sqft on one level with over 80 stores, a 450-seat food court as well as restaurants and a seven-screen Sony-Loews Cineplex, now operated by Zurich Cinemas (independent company).

The mall was purchased by Kohan Retail Investment Group in January 2014, and was later sold to Via Properties in June 2015.

In 2016 Via Properties renamed the mall to Via Port Rotterdam
