Pondville Cemetery

It was established c. 1757, when Norfolk was still part of Wrentham, and now abuts the town line of the two communities.

It was established to serve the residents of the Pondville village, which was first settled in the 1730s.

The cemetery is laid out on a series of terraces that rise from Everett Street to the west, with the oldest graves in the southwest corner, near the Wrentham line.
