List of Teen Titans episodes

"Teen Titans" is an American animated television series based on the DC comics series of the same name by Bob Haney and Bruno Premiani.

Developed by David Slack for Cartoon Network, the series was produced by DC Entertainment and Warner Bros.

Animation with Sander Schwartz serving as executive producer and Glen Murakami, Bruce Timm, and Linda M. Steiner signing on as producers.
