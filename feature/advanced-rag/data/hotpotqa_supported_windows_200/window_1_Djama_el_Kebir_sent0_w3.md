Djamaâ el Kebir

The Great Mosque of Algiers (Arabic: الجامع الكبير‎ ‎ , "Jemaa Kebir") or “Djama’a al-Kebir” (meaning Great Mosque) is a mosque in Algiers, Algeria, located very close to Algiers Harbor.

An inscription on the minbar (منبر) or the pulpit testifies to fact that the mosque was built in 1097.
