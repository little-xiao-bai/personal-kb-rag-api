Bjarkøy Fixed Link

The bridge to Grytøya would be 300 m long plus a 900 m long causeway.

The project also includes 3 km of road on Grytøya to connect the existing roads to the new undersea tunnel.

The Bjarkøy Tunnel will be part of Norwegian County Road 867, while the Sandsøya Bridge will be part of Norwegian County Road 124.
