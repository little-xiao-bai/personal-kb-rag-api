Aldosterone

Aldosterone, the main mineralocorticoid hormone, is a steroid hormone produced by the zona glomerulosa of the adrenal cortex in the adrenal gland.

It is essential for sodium conservation in the kidney, salivary glands, sweat glands and colon.

It plays a central role in the regulation of the plasma sodium (Na), the extracellular potassium (K) and arterial blood pressure.
