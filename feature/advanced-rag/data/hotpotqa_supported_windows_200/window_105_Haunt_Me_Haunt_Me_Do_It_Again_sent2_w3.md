Haunt Me, Haunt Me Do It Again

The album mixes the digital signal processing of glitch with post-rock structures and melodies.

The sounds used for this album, as well as most of Tim Hecker’s other works, originate from a guitar, piano, and laptop.

The title of the song “The Work of Art in the Age of Cultural Overproduction” is a reference to Walter Benjamin's essay, "The Work of Art in the Age of Mechanical Reproduction".
