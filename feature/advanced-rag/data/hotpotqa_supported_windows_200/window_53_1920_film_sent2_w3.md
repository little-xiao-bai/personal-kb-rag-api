1920 (film)

Filmed in Hindi, the film revolves around the events surrounding a married couple living in a haunted house in the year 1920.

The film stars debutant actors Rajneesh Duggal and Adah Sharma as the married couple and Indraneil Sengupta in a special role.

The film is the first installment of the 1920 (film series), which was a critical and commercial success.
