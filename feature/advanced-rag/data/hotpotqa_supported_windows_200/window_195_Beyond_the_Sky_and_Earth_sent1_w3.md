Beyond the Sky and Earth

Beyond the Sky and Earth or Beyond the Sky and the Earth: A Journey into Bhutan is a memoir written by Jamie Zeppa of her experience working as a lecturer in English at the Sherubtse College near Trashigang in eastern Bhutan.

Zeppa took up an assignment for two years.

Initially she started writing a fiction based on her experience there, but she was suggested to write it in memoir form and she took the advice.
