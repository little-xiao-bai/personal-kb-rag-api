Rani Mukerji filmography

Rani Mukerji is an Indian actress known for her work in Bollywood films.

She made her screen debut with a supporting role in "Biyer Phool" (1996), a Bengali film directed by her father Ram Mukherjee.

Her first leading role was that of a rape victim in the 1997 social drama "Raja Ki Aayegi Baraat", for which she won a special jury trophy at the Screen Awards ceremony.
