Xin Zhui

Xin Zhui (; died 163 BC), also known as Lady Dai or Marquise of Dai, was the wife of Li Cang (利蒼 ), the Marquis of Dai, during the Han dynasty (206 BC – 220 AD).

She gained fame more than 2,000 years after her death, when her tomb was discovered inside a hill known as Mawangdui, in Changsha, Hunan, China.
