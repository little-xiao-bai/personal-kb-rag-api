Rob Dougan

He is known primarily for his breakthrough 1995 single "Clubbed to Death (Kurayamino Variation)", further popularised by 1999's "The Matrix" soundtrack.

"Clubbed to Death" was re-released on his debut album "Furious Angels" in 2002, seven years after its initial release.

As well as providing several Clubbed To Death Variations most notably Kurayamino Variation he has also provided a Clubbed To Death Variation of the Moby classic Porcelain.
