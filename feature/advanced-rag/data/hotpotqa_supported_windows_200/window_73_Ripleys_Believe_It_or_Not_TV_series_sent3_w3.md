Ripley's Believe It or Not! (TV series)

The first series aired on NBC from 1949 to 1950, and was hosted by Robert L. Ripley until his death, after which several substitute hosts filled in.

The series was revived for ABC in the 1980s, and was hosted primarily by Jack Palance.

Another revival debuted on TBS in 2000, and aired until 2003, with Dean Cain as host.
