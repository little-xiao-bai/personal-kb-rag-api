Erika Jayne

Erika Girardi (born 10 July 1971), known professionally as Erika Jayne, is an American singer, actress and television personality.

Raised in Atlanta, Girardi came to prominence with the release of her debut single "Roller Coaster", which topped the US "Billboard" Dance Club Songs chart in 2007; it was included on her debut studio album "Pretty Mess" in 2009.
