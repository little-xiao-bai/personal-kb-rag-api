Old 8×10

All of these except "Promises" reached Number One on the Hot Country Songs charts in the late 1980s.

The German edition of the album (the first Randy Travis release in that country) contained the bonus track "Forever and Ever, Amen".

In January 1990, Old 8×10 earned Travis three American Music Awards for 'Favorite Country Male Artist', 'Favorite Country Album', and 'Favorite Country Single' (Deeper Than the Holler).
