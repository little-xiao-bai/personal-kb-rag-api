Alan Cransberg

Cransberg grew up in Bunbury, Western Australia, attending Bunbury Senior High School.

He played for the South Bunbury Football Club in the South West Football League (SWFL), before being recruited by the Swan Districts Football Club in the Perth-based Western Australian National Football League (WANFL) prior to the 1976 season.

Cransberg went on to play 115 games for Swan Districts, mainly playing as a defender.
