History of the Washington Senators (1901–60)

For a time, from 1911 to 1933, the Senators were one of the more successful franchises in Major League Baseball.

The team's rosters included Baseball Hall of Fame members Goose Goslin, Sam Rice, Joe Cronin, Bucky Harris, Heinie Manush and one of the greatest players and pitchers of all time, Walter Johnson.

But the Senators are remembered more for their many years of mediocrity and futility, including six last-place finishes in the 1940s and 1950s.
