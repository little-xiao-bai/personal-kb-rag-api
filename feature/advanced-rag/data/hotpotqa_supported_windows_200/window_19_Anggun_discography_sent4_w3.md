Anggun discography

Anggun's popularity was later established after the success of the single "Mimpi", which was listed as one of the 150 Greatest Indonesian Songs of All Time.

With the release of subsequent singles and three more studio albums— "Anak Putih Abu Abu", "Nocturno" and "Anggun C. Sasmi... Lah!!!

"—Anggun became one of the most successful Indonesian rock singers in the early 1990s.
