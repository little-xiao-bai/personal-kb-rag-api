Citadel Mall

It opened on July 29, 1981 and is located at the intersection of Sam Rittenberg Boulevard (SC Hwy.

7) and I-526.

The mall features more than 100 stores, including six anchor stores: the area's largest Belk and Dillard's department stores, Dick's Sporting Goods, Sears and the region's first Target that was recently remodeled to include a new "Fresh Grocery" section.
