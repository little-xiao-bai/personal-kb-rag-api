Waimea Canyon State Park

Waimea Canyon, also known as the "Grand Canyon of the Pacific", is a large canyon, approximately ten miles (16 km) long and up to 3,000 feet (900 m) deep, located on the western side of Kauaʻ i in the Hawaiian Islands of the United States.

Waimea is Hawaiian for "reddish water", a reference to the erosion of the canyon's red soil.
