Ready to Die

The label's first release, the album features production by Bad Boy founder Sean "Puffy" Combs, Easy Mo Bee, Chucky Thompson, DJ Premier and Lord Finesse, among others.

Recording for the album took place during 1993-94 at The Hit Factory and D&D Studios in New York City.

The partly autobiographical album tells the story of B.I.G.'s experiences as a young criminal.
