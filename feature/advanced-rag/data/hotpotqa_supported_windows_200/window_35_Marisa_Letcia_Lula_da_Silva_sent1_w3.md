Marisa Letícia Lula da Silva

Marisa Letícia Lula da Silva (née Rocco Casa; São Bernardo do Campo, 7 April 1950 – São Paulo, 3 February 2017) was the second wife of former President of Brazil, Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva, and First Lady of Brazil from 2003 to 2010.

Lula's first wife, Maria de Lourdes da Silva, died in labour when Lula was in his twenties.

And Marisa'a first spouse, Marcos Cláudio da Silva, died in 1971.
