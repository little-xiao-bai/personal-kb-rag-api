List of Romeo × Juliet episodes

The series was produced jointly by Gonzo and SKY Perfect Well Think, with Fumitoshi Oizaki as the main director.

It was first broadcast in Japan on Chubu-Nippon Broadcasting between April 4, 2007 and September 26, 2007.

It was later broadcast by other Japanese television networks such as TBS, KBS Kyōto and SUN-TV.
