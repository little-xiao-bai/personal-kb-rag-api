Mactan

The island is part of Cebu Province and it is divided into Lapu-Lapu City and the municipality of Cordova.

The island is separated from Cebu by the Mactan Channel which is crossed by two bridges: the Marcelo Fernan Bridge and the Mactan-Mandaue Bridge.

The island covers some 65 km2 and is home to some 470,000 people, making it the nation's most densely populated island.
