Bart Gets Hit by a Car

Prompted by ambulance-chasing lawyer Lionel Hutz and quack doctor Dr. Nick Riviera, the Simpsons sue Mr. Burns, seeking extensive damages for Bart's injuries.

Hutz and Dr. Nick exaggerate Bart's injuries so they can gain sympathy at the trial.

Marge is against the whole thing and grows concerned with the fact that Homer is asking Bart to lie.
