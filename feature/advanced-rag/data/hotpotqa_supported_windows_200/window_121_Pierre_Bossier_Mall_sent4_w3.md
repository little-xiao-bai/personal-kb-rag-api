Pierre Bossier Mall

It is currently owned and managed by Rouse Properties, one of the largest mall owners in the United States.

Its anchor stores are J. C. Penney, Sears, Dillard's, Virginia College, and Forever 21, formerly Stage, established with the sale in 1994 by Horace Ladymon of the Beall-Ladymon Corporation.

The mall had a theater, The Bossier 6.
