Hot in Cleveland (season 1)

The series stars Valerie Bertinelli, Wendie Malick and Jane Leeves as the three Los Angeles women Melanie Moretti, Victoria Chase, and Joy Scroggs.

Veteran actress Betty White also stars in the series as Elka Ovstrosky, the women's sassy caretaker.

The season also featured numerous guest stars, including John Schneider, Susan Lucci, Carl Reiner and Joe Jonas.
