HP 64000

The HP 64000, introduced circa 1980, is a tool for developing hardware and software for products based on commercial microcomputers.

The earliest commercial 64000 development systems contain from one to six 64100A Development Stations sharing a hard drive and line printer.

This arrangement allowed up to six designers and engineers to use their workstations together, sharing the HPIB instrumentation bus and a hard drive and a line printer.
