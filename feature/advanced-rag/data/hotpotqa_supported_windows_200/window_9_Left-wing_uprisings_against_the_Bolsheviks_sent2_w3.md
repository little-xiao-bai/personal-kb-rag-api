Left-wing uprisings against the Bolsheviks

They were led or supported by left-wing groups such as some factions of the Socialist Revolutionary Party, Left Socialist Revolutionaries, Mensheviks, and anarchists.

The uprisings started in 1918 and continued during and after the Civil War until around 1924.

The Bolsheviks increasingly abandoned attempts to invite these groups to join the government and instead suppressed them with force.
