2007 Cleveland Browns season

The season began with the Browns attempting to improve upon their 4–12 record from the 2006 season, in which the team finished in fourth place in the AFC North.

The Browns also attempted to overcome the many injuries that plagued the team throughout the 2006 season.

The Browns remained under the supervision of head coach Romeo Crennel and they played all of their home games in Cleveland Browns Stadium in Cleveland, Ohio.
